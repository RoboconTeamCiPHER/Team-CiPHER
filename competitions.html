COMPETITION HISTORY

From DD Robocon to a growing robotics circuit.

Team CiPHER's competition journey spans DD Robocon from 2020 onward and spans DD Robocon, other robotics competitions, and RoboQuest — an event organized by Team CiPHER in 2026.

DD Robocon 2020

THEMEROBO RUGBY 7s

The 2020 ABU Robocon challenge, hosted in Suva, Fiji, translated Rugby 7s into a two-robot engineering problem. A Pass Robot worked with a Try Robot to transfer the ball, score a Try and attempt the distinctive Goal Kick over the conversion bar. The theme emphasized coordinated motion, ball handling and precise kicking. citeturn1search22

DD Robocon 2023

THEMECASTING FLOWERS OVER ANGKOR WAT

The 2023 challenge in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, was inspired by Angkor Wat and Cambodian folklore. Two robots — a Rabbit Robot and an Elephant Robot — cooperated in a ring-toss game, casting team-coloured rings toward poles around an Angkor Wat-inspired field. DD Robocon 2024

THEMEHARVEST DAY

Harvest Day, hosted in Vietnam, transformed the rice-farming cycle into a robotics challenge. The two-robot teams had to reproduce tasks inspired by planting, harvesting and transporting grain on a terraced-field-inspired arena. The design problem therefore combined manipulation, transport, positioning and reliable coordination. citeturn2search3turn2search11

DD Robocon 2025

THEMEROBOT BASKETBALL

Robot Basketball, hosted in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, placed two robots on a basketball court where teams alternated between offence and defence. Robots could pass and dribble, take normal shots and attempt high-value dunks while defending their own basket. The competition explicitly tied scoring performance to teamwork, precision and strategy. citeturn2search2turn2search49

DD Robocon 2026

THEMEKUNG FU QUEST

Kung Fu Quest, hosted in Hong Kong, combines Chinese martial-arts traditions with a two-robot engineering challenge. R1 can be manual or autonomous, while R2 must operate autonomously. The robots collaborate to assemble weapons, collect Kung-Fu Scrolls in the Meihua Forest and compete in a Giant Tic-Tac-Toe-style final challenge.
