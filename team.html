TEAM

The people behind the machines.

Founded in 2018, Team CiPHER is a multidisciplinary robotics team organized across mechanical, electronics, programming and administration. Explore the leadership and member structure of each competition cycle.

Team domains.

Four domains working together to design, build and compete.
