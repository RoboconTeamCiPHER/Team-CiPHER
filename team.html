The people behind the machines.

Founded in 2018, Team CiPHER brings mechanical, electronics, programming and administration together to build competitive robots.

01 · DESIGN & BUILDMechanical3D CAD, structural design, fabrication, assembly, GD&T and FEA for competition-ready robot mechanisms.CAD · Manufacturing · Simulation
02 · HARDWAREElectronicsEmbedded systems, PCB design, wiring, sensors, actuators, power distribution and real-time control.PCB · Embedded · Control
03 · INTELLIGENCEProgrammingROS 2 integration, robot control, computer vision, localization, SLAM and autonomous navigation.ROS 2 · Vision · Autonomy
04 · OPERATIONSAdministrationProject coordination, sponsorship, documentation, outreach, event management and team operations.Planning · Outreach · Delivery
