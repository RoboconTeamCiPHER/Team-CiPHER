TEAM

The people behind the machines.

Founded in 2018, Team CiPHER is a 20+ member robotics team organized across four domains. Every build needs all four working together.

Mechanical

CAD, mechanisms, analysis, fabrication.

04

Operations, documentation, outreach, coordination.
