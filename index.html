We build robotic systems.

Team CiPHER is a multidisciplinary robotics team focused on designing, building, programming, testing and refining robotic systems for competitions, experimentation and research. The lab sits inside the Automation and Robotics building at RSCOE, but the team operates independently of any single department.

01

Mechanical

CAD, mechanism design, structural analysis and fabrication of competition and research robots.

02

Electronics

Embedded systems, sensors, actuators, power distribution and low-level control.

03

Programming

ROS 2, autonomy, computer vision and the software that ties hardware together.

04

Administration

Operations, documentation, outreach and the coordination that keeps builds on schedule.
