COMPETITION · ROBOFEST

VARUNA — Vision-Guided Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV)

VARUNA is a compact, fully autonomous underwater vehicle designed for reliable operation in low-visibility aquatic environments. Its architecture combines a waterproof PVC pressure hull, a transparent vision dome, and a six-thruster holonomic propulsion system for precise navigation, perception and underwater manipulation.

COMPUTE

NVIDIA Jetson Nano for high-level vision, autonomy and AI inference.
STM32 Nucleo-64 for real-time motor control, PID loops and low-latency I/O.

SENSORS

Underwater HD camera · BNO055 IMU · Bar30 pressure sensor · dual hydrophones for TDOA-based acoustic localization.

ACTUATION

Six BLDC thrusters for full 3D holonomic maneuvering · worm–spur gear gripper for underwater object interaction.

POWER

14.8V Li-Po propulsion battery with isolated power banks for Jetson Nano and STM32.

AUTONOMY STACK

ROS 2 / Nav2 for mission planning and autonomous navigation · OpenCV + transformer-based detectors for gate, mat and object recognition · EKF/UKF multi-sensor fusion · PID stabilization for depth, pitch, roll and thrust · Behavior-tree navigation for modular mission execution.

APPLICATIONS

Underwater structure inspection · environmental and ecological monitoring · marine research and sampling · autonomous underwater robotics competitions.

CURRENT STATUS

Completed: system ideation, architecture and subsystem design, waterproofing and buoyancy strategy, sensor pipeline design and project timeline. The next phase requires funding support for fabrication, hardware integration and controlled pool testing.
