Team CiPHER

Official Robotics Team of JSPM's RSCOE

An independent, student-run robotics lab in the Automation & Robotics building at RSCOE, Pune—designing, building, and programming autonomous systems for national competitions and applied research.

Who we are.

Operating across four integrated divisions, we take robots from initial concept and CAD to embedded hardware and autonomous control. Meet the members on our Team page.

Mechanical

  • Parametric CAD & mechanism design
  • Structural FEA & lightweight builds
  • Precision fabrication & assembly
