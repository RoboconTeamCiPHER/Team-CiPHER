We build robotic systems.

Founded in 2018, Team CiPHER is a multidisciplinary robotics team focused on designing, building, programming, testing and refining robotic systems for competitions, experimentation and research. The lab sits inside the Automation and Robotics building at RSCOE, but the team operates independently of any single department.

Mechanical

  • CAD and 3D modelling
  • Mechanism and system design
  • Structural analysis and FEA
  • Fabrication and manufacturing
  • Robot integration and testing

Electronics

  • Embedded systems
  • Sensors and actuators
  • Power distribution
  • Motor and actuator control
  • Low-level hardware integration

Programming

  • ROS 2 and robotic software
  • Autonomous navigation
  • Computer vision
  • Simulation and digital robotics
  • Software-hardware integration

Administration

  • Team operations and coordination
  • Documentation and records
  • Outreach and communications
  • Event and competition management
  • Planning and scheduling
