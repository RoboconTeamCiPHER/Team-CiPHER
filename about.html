ABOUT TEAM CIPHER

We don't just enter competitions. We engineer robotic systems.

Founded in 2018, Team CiPHER is a multidisciplinary student robotics team from JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering, Pune, working across mechanical engineering, electronics, robotics programming, computer vision, autonomous robotics, simulation, competitive robotics and research & development. Today, the team has 20+ members and participates in many robotics competitions.

Who we are.

Team CiPHER's laboratory is located in the Automation and Robotics building at RSCOE.

The team is an independent, multidisciplinary student organization — not a department or sub-department of the college.

The team was formed in 2018 to take robotics beyond the classroom: full systems built from concept through CAD, simulation, fabrication, electronics, software, integration, testing and iteration.

That process is the same whether the outcome is a competition robot, a simulation stack, or an internal tool used to speed up the next build.
