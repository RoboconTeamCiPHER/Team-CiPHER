Since 2018

Team CiPHER

Official Robotics Team of JSPM's RSCOE

An independent, student-run robotics lab in the Automation & Robotics building at RSCOE, Pune—building autonomous systems for national competitions and applied research.

How we work together.

Four specialized divisions collaborate end-to-end on every machine. Visit Team for member rosters and Projects for active builds.

Mechanical

  • Parametric CAD & mechanism design
  • Structural FEA & lightweight builds
  • Fabrication, assembly & testing

Mechanical domain

Electronics

  • Embedded hardware & power buses
  • Motor control & actuator drives
  • Sensor integration & bring-up

Electronics domain

Programming

  • ROS 2 packages & Gazebo simulation
  • Autonomous navigation & vision
  • Hardware-software integration

Programming domain

Administration

  • Operations, budgets & schedules
  • Documentation & outreach
  • Event & competition logistics
