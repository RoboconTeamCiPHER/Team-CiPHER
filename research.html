RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Explore. Experiment. Engineer.

Team CiPHER explores robotics through simulation, autonomous systems, embedded control and practical engineering experiments.

Research domains

Areas where our team investigates methods, builds prototypes and tests ideas.

01

Autonomous Robotics

ROS 2, localization, SLAM, path planning, navigation and multi-sensor integration.

02

Simulation & Digital Prototyping

Robot description packages, Gazebo simulation, virtual testing and model-based development.

03

Computer Vision

Vision-based perception, object and pose detection, camera pipelines and visual feedback.

04

Embedded Systems & Control

Microcontrollers, sensor fusion, actuator control and hardware-software integration.

From hypothesis to hardware

Research is approached as an iterative engineering process, from problem definition to validation.

02Model & simulate
03Build & integrate
